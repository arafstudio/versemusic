# Verse Music



![Verse Music](https://i.postimg.cc/MHLPnFHf/01-library.png)



**Your music in one place.**

Verse Music is an open source, offline-first music app by **Araf Studio**. Library, playlists, favorites, offline downloads, a floating Dynamic Island player, song suggestions and bug reports, all in one clean dark interface.

Website: [versemusic.pages.dev](https://versemusic.pages.dev)

---

## Library

Your whole collection in one place: Overview, All Songs, Artists and Albums, with fast search. Jump straight into the featured playlist, shuffle everything with one tap, or pick up from Recently Played.

## Playlists



![Playlists](https://i.postimg.cc/Y2PbfT2L/02-playlists.webp)



The featured playlist, **AR'AF'S PE'ACE**, holds 178 songs from V1 to V4 and is highlighted everywhere in the app. Smart playlists like Recently Played build themselves as you listen. Open Spotify, Download all, Play all and Shuffle are one tap away.

## Browse by Artist



![Artists](https://i.postimg.cc/0rgGRRrZ/03-artists.webp)



Songs by the same artist are grouped together. Songs load quickly in the background while you browse.

## Favorites



![Favorites](https://i.postimg.cc/rm3ZmHKY/04-favorites.webp)



Heart a song and it stays one tap away. A smooth mini player stays with you on every screen.

## Download Once, Play Offline



![Downloads](https://i.postimg.cc/XJGxTgQF/05-downloads.webp)



Streaming never fills your storage. Only the songs you save use space. Play all or delete all saved songs from one screen.

## Dynamic Island



![Dynamic Island](https://i.postimg.cc/nL7Cgjqh/06-dynamic-island.png)



A floating player that glows with your album art. It floats over any app, so you can control your music from anywhere on your phone.

- Compact and expanded modes
- Cover-colour glow
- Play, pause and skip

## Report a Bug



![Report a bug](https://i.postimg.cc/cHx0hrm9/07-report-bug.png)



Something not right? Describe what happened and what you expected, then send the report from Settings. Reports go out under your username, with one tap to send.

## Suggest a Song



![Suggest a song](https://i.postimg.cc/Y0T5591y/08-suggest-song.png)



Request any song from Settings by adding the song name and artist name. If we like it, it is added to AR'AF'S PE'ACE.

## Features

- Library with Overview, All Songs, Artists and Albums
- Fast search
- Featured playlist and smart playlists
- Favorites
- Offline downloads
- Browse by artist
- Floating Dynamic Island player
- Mini player on every screen
- Song suggestions
- In-app bug reports

## Requirements

- Android 7.0 or higher

## Open Source

Verse Music is open source. Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue or send a pull request, contributions are welcome.
